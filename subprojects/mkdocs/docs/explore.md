# Ground Rupture Explorer

<div id="gre-explore" markdown="1">
**Use simulations to understand ground rupture.**

Describe the site conditions and fault movement you want to
investigate, then see the simulated results. This is a research
tool; it does not predict what will happen at a particular site.

The interactive explorer needs JavaScript and a supported browser.
Use a current stable version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
If the controls do not appear, update your browser and reload. For
Safari on iPhone or iPad, update iOS or iPadOS.

Your work stays in this tab, including across a reload. Closing the
tab or opening a new one starts a fresh scenario. Supporting links in
the interactive explorer open a separate tab to preserve your work.

Read [the paper](paper.md), [data and citation](data.md),
[the glossary](glossary.md), or [the dashboards](index.md).
The [existing explorer](scenario.md) remains available separately.
See the [full disclaimer](#gre-explore-disclaimer) below.

<noscript><p>The interactive controls and results need JavaScript,
which is turned off or unavailable in this browser.</p></noscript>
</div>
